How Do I Find the Inverse of a 2x2 Matrix?

For [[a,b],[c,d]], the inverse is (1/(ad-bc)) * [[d,-b],[-c,a]]. Worked example with step-by-step formula for quick calculation.

How Do I Calculate a 3x3 Determinant?

Cofactor expansion along the first row: det = a(ei-fh) - b(di-fg) + c(dh-eg). Step-by-step formula and worked example.

What Are the Rules for Matrix Multiplication?

A(m*n) * B(n*p) = C(m*p). Inner dimensions must match. Element C[i][j] = sum of A[i][k] * B[k][j] for all k.

How Do I Choose K in K-Means Clustering?

Plot WCSS against K, find the elbow, and check it with stability and silhouette. Measured WCSS curve and the limits of the method.

What Is the Difference Between Vector Mode and Text Mode?

Vector mode compares numbers directly. Text mode counts raw term frequencies first. Worked example shows why stopwords move the score.

How Are FLOPs Estimated for a Convolution?

FLOPs = 2 x kH x kW x C_in x out_H x out_W x C_out. Derivation, a worked 3x3 example, and what the estimate leaves out.
